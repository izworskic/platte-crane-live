# Sources

## Crane abundance
**Crane Trust** is the observational anchor. Historical data are based primarily on the Crane Trust report *Spring Aerial Sandhill Crane Counts of the Central Platte River Valley, Nebraska: Weekly Public Updates from 2016 to 2025*, including Appendix A bias-corrected weekly estimates. Verified 2026 public updates are appended with provenance.

The UI calls these official survey abundance indices/estimates, never real-time censuses.

## Weather and hazards
National Weather Service API (`api.weather.gov`) for point/hourly forecasts and active alerts across Grand Island, Gibbon/Rowe, and Kearney.

## River
USGS Water Services instantaneous values for gauges 06768000, 06770200, 06770500 and parameters 00060/00065/00010 where available.

## Solar
Locally computed NOAA-style sunrise/sunset and civil twilight geometry; no paid astronomy API.

## Maps / access
MapLibre with public basemap styling and versioned public-viewing-site data. Access details are deliberately conservative where not independently verified.
