# Wildlife ethics

The optimization objective is trip usefulness **subject to wildlife protection**.

- Use designated public viewing sites.
- Respect private land and closures.
- Never approach feeding flocks or river roosts.
- Never stop in active roadways.
- Avoid flash or disturbance around roosting birds where prohibited.
- The application does not publish private roost coordinates or infer fine-grained roost locations from vague narrative reports.

Distribution narratives are intentionally kept coarse unless an authoritative source explicitly publishes safe public-location detail.
